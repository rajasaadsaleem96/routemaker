Panorra LKH Route Optimizer

A native Windows 10/11 x64 desktop application for routing against the Panorra directed Uint16 distance-matrix format.

Optimizer engine:
LKH-3 by Keld Helsgaun (version 3.0.13).
The LKH source and engine remain subject to their original licensing terms. Commercial use/distribution may require permission from the author.

The application expects:
- distance-matrix.uint16
- gps-mapping.txt
- matrix-info.txt

Matrix layout:
row-major, little-endian Uint16Array, directed distances in meters.
